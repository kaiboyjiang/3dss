GVCSG GENERIC VIBE CODED SPACE GAME

You are the pilot of the Valkyrie, a Caldera-class assault frigate docked at Ardent Relay Station. Corsair pirates have been raiding the asteroid belt. Hunt them down, spend the bounties on new hulls and outfits at Helion Orbital Shipyard, then fly to the jump gates to chart new systems. Federation space is patrolled by the Helion Navy; pirate systems are lawless.
